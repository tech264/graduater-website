# GRADUATER launch checklist

The website is frontend-only. Forty public pages are available, including all nine destinations, nine course categories, illustrative university profiles and six educational articles.

## Information awaiting the owner
- Original GRADUATER logo: only the text brief was uploaded. The header currently shows the brand name, not a recreated logo.
- Verified email, phone, WhatsApp number, office address and social links.
- Verified universities, course availability, tuition and scholarship details.
- Approved team biographies and genuine student stories with permission to publish.
- Business/legal review of the clearly marked initial privacy and terms drafts.

## Enquiries
Forms validate required name, email, phone and education. They prepare a local text download and explicitly do not claim delivery. No enquiry data is sent or persistently stored. Configure real delivery before changing this behaviour.

## Publishing and SEO
Unique titles, descriptions and social metadata exist on each content route. Homepage organisation and article structured data are present. No absolute hero image is tagged because bundled/CDN pointer images use relative URLs.

A published public domain is not yet available. Sitemap, canonical URLs and absolute breadcrumb structured data are deferred until that domain is known. Never use the Lovable preview domain as the public canonical origin. Preserve existing robots directives.

## Verification
Nine retained tests cover routing, destination coverage, medical programme exclusions, combined university filters and honest enquiry handling. Browser checks exercised homepage search to university discovery, country detail navigation, course and article filtering, FAQ, story carousel, enquiry draft download, mobile menus and all 40 public pages. No browser errors or horizontal overflow were observed.
